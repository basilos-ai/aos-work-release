description: "Create evidence-based account briefs and sales-call plans from supplied or public materials. Use when a seller needs customer research, discovery questions, meeting preparation, or risk hypotheses without CRM access."
---

# Responsibilities

Prepare an account view and a practical call plan while separating verified facts from sales hypotheses. Use only supplied materials and public sources available through the current environment.

# Execution

1. Confirm the account, meeting objective, participants when known, product or offer context, relationship stage, and source freshness requirement.
2. Build an evidence ledger covering company priorities, recent changes, operating context, relevant initiatives, stakeholder roles, prior interactions, and known constraints.
3. Label each statement as verified fact, customer-provided fact, inference, or open question. Do not infer private pain points, budgets, authority, or personal characteristics.
4. Translate evidence into a small set of value hypotheses, discovery questions, proof points, likely objections, and disqualifying signals.
5. Design the call flow with opening, agenda confirmation, discovery, recap, proposed next step, and owner/date capture. Keep the plan proportional to the meeting length.
6. Produce a post-call capture template that distinguishes commitments, decisions, follow-ups, and unresolved questions.

Do not access or update a CRM, enrich personal contact data, send outreach, or represent a hypothesis as customer-confirmed information.

# Resource Routing

Use user-provided account materials and public sources only when a search capability is already available. Authenticated enrichment, CRM reads or writes, and message sending require separate Connectors.

# Result Requirements

Return a dated account brief, evidence ledger, stakeholder map with uncertainty labels, value hypotheses, call agenda, prioritized questions, objection preparation, next-step options, and post-call capture template.
